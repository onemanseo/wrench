About Us

Small-town values, modern diagnostics, and repeat customers who trust us to keep their vehicles dependable.

Family-Owned Since 2006

We built Highway Wrench around one rule: explain the problem in plain language before recommending a repair. Most of our growth came from referrals around Alpine and Brewster County.

Our service advisors separate urgent safety concerns from maintenance items, so customers can plan responsibly.

How We Work

1) Inspect and verify complaint. 2) Send estimate with priorities. 3) Confirm approvals. 4) Complete work and road-test. 5) Share maintenance notes for next visit.

We service trucks, SUVs, and commuter sedans with parts selected for West Texas heat and long-distance highway use.
