Our Story

From a one-bay startup in 2006 to a trusted local shop, we built this business one honest repair at a time.

Highway Wrench Auto Works: Built in Alpine

Highway Wrench Auto Works was founded in 2006 by Daniel Mercer, a West Texas technician who started with one lift, one toolbox, and a commitment to explain repairs clearly. Early years were built on repeat ranch and commuter customers who wanted dependable work without dealership overhead.

As Alpine grew, we invested in additional bays, scan tools, and ASE training. Through heat waves, parts shortages, and changing vehicle tech, our operating rule never changed: diagnose accurately, communicate clearly, and stand behind the repair.

Today we remain independently owned and community-rooted, serving Alpine and Brewster County with practical, warranty-backed service.
