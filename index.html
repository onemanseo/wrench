Our Service Process

1) You Contact Us

Call, text, email, or submit a request. During business hours we respond within about 1-2 hours.

2) Initial Consultation

We review symptoms, maintenance history, and urgency. Phone consult is always no-charge.

3) Diagnostic Inspection

Vehicle goes on lift, systems are tested, and root cause is identified instead of clearing symptoms.

4) Estimate & Explanation

You receive a detailed estimate and plain-language explanation of priorities and options.

5) Your Approval

No repair starts until you approve parts and labor. No surprise add-ons.

6) Repair & Quality Check

Work is completed to ASE standards and verified before release.

7) Vehicle Return

We review completed work, provide documentation, and explain warranty coverage.

Frequently Asked Questions

How long will my repair take?

Oil changes are usually 30-45 minutes. Diagnostics are typically 1-2 hours. Most common repairs are completed same day after approval.

Do you work on all makes and models?

Yes. Domestic and foreign vehicles including Ford, Chevy, Dodge, Toyota, Honda, Nissan, Subaru, Hyundai, and more.

What payment methods do you accept?

Cash, check, debit, Visa, Mastercard, and American Express. Financing options are available for larger repairs through partner lenders.

Can you help after hours?

Yes. We offer after-hours drop-off by arrangement so your vehicle can be inspected first thing next morning.

What warranty do you provide?

12 months or 12,000 miles on parts and labor (whichever comes first) for qualifying repairs.

Do you offer fleet discounts?

Yes, for ranches, contractors, and small business fleets. Contact us for a custom maintenance and pricing plan.
