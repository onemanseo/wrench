Privacy Policy

Last updated: April 26, 2026. This policy explains how Highway Wrench Auto Works handles customer information.

Information We Collect

We may collect your name, phone number, email address, vehicle details, and service history when you contact us or schedule repair work.

How We Use Information

We use your information to provide estimates, schedule service, send repair updates, and maintain warranty-related documentation.

We apply practical safeguards to protect business records and service communications. We keep records only as long as needed for operations, legal compliance, and warranty support.

Your Choices
