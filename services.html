Complete Service Menu

Comprehensive mechanical service with clear diagnostics, realistic timelines, and transparent pricing before work begins.

Engine Diagnostics and Repair

Check Engine Light Diagnostics

Most warning lights are not immediate emergencies, but ignoring them usually increases cost. We use professional scanners, live data, and targeted testing to identify the root cause.

  • Oxygen sensor and catalytic converter analysis
  • Mass airflow and fuel-trim checks
  • Misfire isolation and ignition testing
  • Emission leak diagnostics

Timeline: 1-2 hours • Starting at $89

Engine Performance Issues

For hard starts, rough idle, power loss, or fuel-economy drops, we pair full-system scans with focused component testing.

  • Spark plug and ignition-coil health
  • Fuel delivery and filter restriction checks
  • Air intake and sensor validation
  • Timing-related symptom analysis

Oil Changes and Fluid Service

  • Correct oil grade and quality filter installation
  • Fluid top-off and visual under-hood review
  • Interval guidance based on driving conditions

Recommended: every 5,000-7,000 miles depending on vehicle usage.

Brake and Suspension Service

Complete Brake Inspection

  • Front/rear pad wear measurement
  • Rotor condition, runout, and scoring check
  • Fluid condition and hydraulic integrity
  • Caliper, hose, and parking-brake validation

Warning signs: soft pedal, grind/squeal, vibration, brake warning lamp.

Brake Pad and Rotor Replacement

We typically replace pads and rotors as a balanced set for predictable stopping and long-term reliability.

  • OEM-quality daily-driver options
  • Premium low-dust / higher-performance options
  • 12-month / 12,000-mile warranty coverage

Suspension and Steering

  • Struts, shocks, and bushing wear diagnosis
  • Tie-rod and steering-stability checks
  • Sway-bar link service and post-repair alignment prep

Common symptoms: clunking, uneven tire wear, pull/drift, unstable ride.

Air Conditioning and Cooling Systems

A/C Diagnostics

  • High/low side pressure testing
  • Leak tracing with dye and visual inspection
  • Compressor clutch and fan operation checks
  • Cabin airflow performance validation

Diagnostic fee: $79 (often credited when repair proceeds).

A/C Recharge and Leak Repair

Recharge-only failures are common, but we also identify and repair leaks when needed.

  • Evacuation and calibrated recharge
  • Dye injection and leak-source confirmation
  • Compressor seal, condenser, and hose leak repair

Typical recharge range: $130-$200

Cooling System Service

  • Coolant condition and level checks
  • Thermostat and water-pump evaluation
  • Radiator and hose integrity review
  • Coolant flush intervals around 30,000 miles when required

Electrical System Service

Battery and Charging System

  • Load test and cold-cranking verification
  • Alternator output and voltage stability testing
  • Terminal corrosion and cable integrity checks

Alternator and Starter Repairs

  • Bench test confirmation before replacement

Lighting and Accessory Circuits

  • Headlight and taillight diagnostics

Transmission Service and Repair

Transmission Maintenance

  • Fluid level, color, and odor assessment
  • Filter and fluid service at proper intervals
  • Leak inspection around pan/seals/lines

Common interval: 30,000-60,000 miles (vehicle dependent).

Transmission Diagnostics

  • Delayed engagement and shift-quality analysis
  • Slip, overheat, and warning-code diagnostics
  • Road test + scan data correlation

Minor Transmission Repairs

  • Seal/gasket leak repair
  • Cooler-line and hose replacement
  • Solenoid-level repairs where applicable

Major rebuilds are coordinated through trusted specialty partners with warranty support.
