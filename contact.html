Scheduling FAQ

How quickly can I get an appointment?

Diagnostics usually open within 1-3 days. Routine maintenance is often available the same week. Urgent issues are prioritized when possible.

Do I need to drop off or can I wait?

Both are available. You can use our waiting area, or we can call when your vehicle is ready.

What should I bring?

Keys, identification, and any recent service records that might help diagnostics.

Will you update me before repairs begin?

Yes. We call after diagnostics, review estimate details, and only begin approved work.
